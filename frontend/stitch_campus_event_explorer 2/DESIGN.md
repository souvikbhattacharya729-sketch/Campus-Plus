## Brand & Style
The design system powers an energetic, punchy, and modern collegiate discovery platform crafted to counter the ubiquitous pastel SaaS aesthetic. The visual tone is active, celebratory, and undeniably campus-native—evoking the electric atmosphere of homecoming tailgates, midnight hackathons, sold-out quad concerts, and pop-up student clubs.

The core style bridges modern high-contrast digital utility with tactile sticker culture:
- **High-Impact Boldness:** Expressive typographic scale, rich saturated surfaces, and definitive contrasts that command attention without inducing visual fatigue.
- **Physical Sticker Metaphor:** Event badges, interest tags, and live status indicators emulate physical tactile die-cut stickers and festival wristbands with crisp edge definition and micro-depth.
- **Energetic Atmosphere:** Layered depth that combines deep blueberry navy foundation tones with electric kinetic highlights, rejecting sterile gray-and-white enterprise layouts in favor of an unmistakable, high-spirited campus identity.

## Colors
The palette leverages high-chroma primary and accent values structured over clean daylight surfaces, framed by deep blueberry-navy neutrals rather than muted slates or cold carbon blacks.

### Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#2563eb` - Electric Cobalt):** Primary actions, focal states, active navigation tabs, high-priority interactive affordances.
- **Secondary (`#f97316` - Energized Tangerine):** High-heat callouts, RSVP urgency, ticket releases, featured headliners, countdown indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#10b981` - Vivid Emerald):** "Happening Now" pulses, confirmed statuses, campus verification badges, open capacity indicators.
- **Neutral Deep (`#0f172a` - Blueberry Navy):** High-contrast headlines, bottom navigation surfaces, hero cards, dark-mode surfaces, and structural anchor blocks.
- **Surface System:**
  - Base canvas: Pure clean white (`#ffffff`) for optimal legibility of dense event data.
  - Surface subtle: Ultra-light blueberry ice (`#f8fafc`) for cards and nested modules.
  - Surface dynamic: Saturated navy (`#0f172a`) used strategically for festival-style highlight banners and night-mode calendar takeovers.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic roles to maintain structural continuity while leaning into its geometric, friendly, and contemporary letterforms.

### Typographic Hierarchies
- **Display & Large Headlines (`display-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Executed in 700 and 800 weights with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver aggressive editorial impact for festival headliners, major campus dates, and hero discovery feeds.
- **Body Text (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`):** Set at regular weight (400) with generous line heights to preserve swift scanning of venues, organizer notes, and schedule breakdowns.
- **Stickers & Badges (`label-sticker`, `label-md`):** Set in 800 weight, uppercase transformation with positive letter-spacing (`0.03em` to `0.05em`) to simulate silkscreened and vinyl collegiate pass markings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses a responsive fluid grid system anchored by an 8pt spatial baseline:
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontally scrolling single-row carousels snap at card boundaries to showcase trending events.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Splits feeds into curated feeds and secondary filter drawers.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout with max container width capped at `1280px` to maintain optimal line lengths. Features an asymmetrical 8-column primary event discovery stream flanked by a 4-column sticky campus social calendar.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation rejects fuzzy, diffuse SaaS dropshadows in favor of crisp, tactile micro-shadows that give UI components the physical presence of collectible tokens and card decks:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base canvas `#ffffff` or muted container `#f8fafc`. Borders defined by subtle, crisp borders (`1px solid #e2e8f0`).
- **Level 1 (Event Cards & Modules):** `0 2px 0 0 #0f172a15, 0 4px 12px -2px #0f172a0d`. Provides immediate visual separation with a grounded tactile edge.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Badges):** `0 4px 0 0 #0f172a, 0 8px 16px -4px #2563eb20`. Creates an energetic, sticker-lifted appearance for primary interaction items.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Bars):** `0 12px 32px -8px #0f172a33, 0 1px 2px 0 #0f172a1a`. Deep atmospheric lift reserved for ticket sheets and filter overlays.
- **Sticker Tactile Effect:** Badges and pill tags layer a `1px` inner white highlight stroke (`inset 0 1px 0 0 #ffffff66`) over high-saturation fills to evoke physical vinyl die-cut stickers.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of 3 (Pill-shaped) defines the expressive signature of the platform.

- **Micro Controls & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for category filters, status indicators, and action buttons.
- **Containers & Cards:** Primary containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`2rem` / `32px`) to evoke large pocket-sized festival passes and digital ticket stubs.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers:** Top edges cut with `rounded-xl` (`3rem` / `48px`) for an organic, sweeping sheet transition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid Electric Cobalt (`#2563eb`) with high-contrast white text (`label-lg`). Box shadow: `0 4px 0 0 #1d4ed8`. On hover, translate Y by `-1px` with shadow expansion. Active state depresses `2px` downward.
- **Secondary Button:** Solid Energized Tangerine (`#f97316`) fill with white text, or deep Blueberry Navy (`#0f172a`) with white text for high-contrast nighttime actions.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** High-contrast outlined pill (`1.5px solid #0f172a`) with transparent fill and active state filled with `#f1f5f9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Sticker Chips:** All-caps (`label-sticker`), thick rounded pill shape, `0.25rem` vertical by `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
- **Live Energy Badge:** Emerald Green (`#10b981`) pill background with an inset pulsing white/emerald beacon dot and dark green text (`#064e3b`).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral light background (`#f1f5f9`) when idle; on selection, switches instantly to Cobalt (`#2563eb`) or Tangerine (`#f97316`) with white text and an extruded tactile micro-shadow.

### Event Cards
- Outer border radius `rounded-lg` (`2rem`).
- Visual anatomy: High-contrast media container topped with die-cut absolute sticker badges (Date, Campus Quad, Price). White or deep navy body container with bold headline typography (`headline-sm`), bold metadata icons, and stacked friend attendee avatars.
- Elevation transitions smoothly from Level 1 to Level 2 on hover, accompanied by a subtle image zoom.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature pill radius (`9999px`) for search and quick-filter fields, or `1rem` radius for multi-line forms.
- Border: `2px solid #e2e8f0`. Focus state: `2px solid #2563eb` with an outer electric glow halo (`0 0 0 4px #2563eb26`). Zero soft-gray ambiguities.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Thicker custom `2px` borders in `#0f172a`. Checkboxes use `rounded-md` (`6px`), radios are circular.
- Selected state fills with Electric Cobalt (`#2563eb`) displaying a crisp, high-contrast white icon.

### Live Campus Ticket Stub (Specialty Component)
- Card divided into a two-part perforated visual layout using dashed CSS border lines and semicircular side cutouts.
- Features prominent QR code placement, dynamic live gate countdowns highlighted in Energized Tangerine (`#f97316`), and real-time door admission indicators in Vivid Emerald (`#10b981`).
